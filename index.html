Shade Finder

Find affordable alternatives to makeup you already own or love.

Upload a photo of a makeup product, like a lipstick, foundation, or blush. Shade Finder tells you:

  • What product it might be
  • The closest shades from other brands, with prices and how close each match is
  • Each product's ingredients, with dyes to watch flagged
  • Whether each brand is cruelty-free
  • Which matches fit what matters to you, like vegan or under a price

How it works

  1. Take or upload a photo of your makeup.
  2. The AI tells you which product it is, lists its ingredients, and suggests similar products from other brands.
  3. You can also tap right on the product's color to match by shade alone.

AI key

Identifying products uses your own Anthropic API key. It's saved only in this browser.

1. Add a photo

  • Use good light, like near a window.
  • Get close so the product fills most of the photo.
  • Keep the product in focus.

Tap directly on the product's color, like the lipstick itself or the foundation in the bottle. Don't tap the packaging or the background. You can tap again to try a different spot.
